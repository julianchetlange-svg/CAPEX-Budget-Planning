Monthly cash flow by project type

Each project's remaining budget (Project Budget minus any Project Total Cost already spent or committed) is divided evenly across its planned spend duration, starting the month the project begins. Figures below reflect only the portion of that spend that falls within the selected budget year. Monthly depreciation by project

In-service date is set to the first day of the month following planned completion. Monthly depreciation is the project budget divided by the depreciation schedule (years × 12), applied straight-line from the in-service month through the end of its useful life.
